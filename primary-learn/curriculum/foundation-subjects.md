# Foundation subjects (KS1–KS2)

Core interactive priority for Primary Path remains **maths** and **english**.  
These subjects are mapped so we can add lighter knowledge/practice packs later without inventing structure.

Sources: individual National Curriculum programmes of study on [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/national-curriculum).

## Computing

| Stage | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| KS1 | Algorithms as instructions; create/debug simple programs; use tech purposefully; recognise common uses of IT; keep personal info private |
| KS2 | Design/write/debug programs; sequence, selection, repetition; variables/input/output; networks (internet); search; use software; responsible use |

**Pathway tip:** start with sequencing instructions (unplugged algorithms) before block coding concepts.

## History

| Stage | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| KS1 | Changes within living memory; events beyond living memory (e.g. Great Fire); lives of significant individuals; significant local history |
| KS2 | Chronology; ancient civilisations; British history overview (Stone Age to 1066 and selected later themes); local history; ancient Greece; non-European society |

**Pathway tip:** KS1 “then and now” before complex chronologies.

## Geography

| Stage | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| KS1 | World/UK/local places; continents & oceans; hot/cold areas; basic vocab for human/physical features; simple maps / aerial photos |
| KS2 | Locate world countries; UK cities/counties/regions; latitudes, hemispheres, tropics; human & physical geography in depth; map skills including 6-figure grid refs, OS symbols |

**Pathway tip:** local area + UK countries before continents/latitudes.

## Art and design

- KS1: materials, drawing, colour, texture; about artists/craftmakers  
- KS2: techniques (drawing, painting, sculpture…); art history; sketchbooks; mastery of materials  

## Design and technology

- KS1: design, make, evaluate simple products; basic cooking & nutrition; structures/mechanisms  
- KS2: more demanding design; mechanical/electrical systems; computing in products; cooking  

## Music

- KS1: sing; play untuned instruments; listen with concentration; experiment with sounds  
- KS2: play/perform; improvise/compose; staff and other notations; history of music; appreciation  

## Physical education

- KS1: master basic movements; team games; simple dance  
- KS2: running/jumping/throwing/catching; competitive games; flexibility/strength; outdoor/adventurous; swimming (by end of KS2, typically)  

Less suited to this tap-based app unless we add movement videos / challenges later.

## Languages (KS2 only)

Statutory from Year 3+. Schools choose the language. Programme covers:

- Listen/speak with increasing confidence  
- Read/write simple phrases  
- Basic grammar appropriate to the language  
- Cultural awareness  

**App note:** only add a language pack if James picks a specific language (e.g. French or Spanish).
