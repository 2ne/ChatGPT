# Science pathway (Year 1 → Year 6)

Source: [National curriculum science programmes of study](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-science-programmes-of-study).

EYFS feeds science via **Understanding the world** (seasons, living things, materials/change) — see [eyfs.md](./eyfs.md).

Science is less strictly “linear” than number bonds, but topics still revisit ideas with more depth (e.g. animals/humans each year). Working scientifically skills grow alongside content.

## Working scientifically (all years)

Build these habits in activities over time:

- Ask simple questions  
- Observe closely; use simple equipment  
- Perform simple tests  
- Identify and classify  
- Use observations to suggest answers  
- Gather/record data to help answer questions  

(KS2 adds fair testing, secondary sources, conclusions, predictions, more precise measurement.)

## Key Stage 1

### Year 1 topics

| Topic | Core ideas |
| --- | --- |
| Plants | Common plants; basic structure (roots, stem, leaves, flowers) |
| Animals including humans | Common animals (fish, amphibians, reptiles, birds, mammals); carnivores/herbivores/omnivores; human body parts & senses |
| Everyday materials | Wood, plastic, glass, metal, water, rock…; physical properties; objects vs materials |
| Seasonal changes | Weather across seasons; day length |

### Year 2 topics

| Topic | Core ideas |
| --- | --- |
| Living things & habitats | Alive / dead / never alive; habitats; simple food chains |
| Plants | Seeds/bulbs grow into mature plants; water, light, temperature |
| Animals including humans | Offspring; basic needs (water, food, air); exercise, food, hygiene |
| Uses of everyday materials | Suitability of materials; squashing, bending, twisting, stretching |

## Lower Key Stage 2

### Year 3

- Plants (functions of parts; requirements for life; water transport; life cycle)  
- Animals including humans (nutrition; skeletons and muscles)  
- Rocks  
- Light (darkness is absence of light; reflection; shadows)  
- Forces and magnets  

### Year 4

- Living things & habitats (grouping; classification keys; environments change)  
- Animals including humans (digestive system; teeth; food chains)  
- States of matter  
- Sound  
- Electricity (simple series circuits; switches; conductors/insulators)  

---

## Upper Key Stage 2

### Year 5

- Living things & habitats (life cycles; reproduction in plants/animals)  
- Animals including humans (human timeline: birth to old age)  
- Properties and changes of materials  
- Earth and space  
- Forces (gravity, air/water resistance, friction, mechanisms)  

### Year 6

- Living things & habitats (classification including microorganisms)  
- Animals including humans (circulatory system; diet, exercise, drugs)  
- Evolution and inheritance  
- Light (travel in straight lines; how we see)  
- Electricity (voltage, brighter/louder, circuit symbols)

1. Y1 animals & body senses

4. Y2 habitats & food chains  
5. Y3 rocks / magnets / light  
6. Y4 states of matter / sound / electricity

8. Y6 evolution (careful, age-appropriate wording)
