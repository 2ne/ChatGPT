# Mathematics pathway (Reception → Year 6)

Source: [National curriculum mathematics programmes of study](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study) + EYFS maths ELGs.

**Aims (DfE):** fluency, mathematical reasoning, problem solving.  
**Progress rule:** only move on when understanding is secure; consolidate earlier material first.

## Domains (threads that run through primary)

1. Number and place value  
2. Addition and subtraction  
3. Multiplication and division  
4. Fractions (→ decimals → percentages)  
5. Measurement  
6. Geometry (shape; position & direction)  
7. Statistics (from Year 2)

For Primary Maths, ship **number threads first** — they unlock almost everything else.

---

## Master number pathway (building blocks)

Read top → bottom. Each step needs the one above.

```text
R  Subitise to 5; count within 10
R  Composition of numbers to 10; bonds to 5; some bonds to 10; doubles
Y1 Count to 100; 1 more/less; read/write to 20
Y1 Number bonds within 10 → within 20; +/− to 20
Y1 Begin ×÷ via arrays / grouping / sharing; halves & quarters
Y2 Place value (tens/ones); bonds to 20 fluent; related facts to 100
Y2 2, 5, 10 tables; +/− two-digit; fractions 1/3, 1/4, 2/4, 3/4
Y3 Place value to 1000; column +/−; 3, 4, 8 tables
Y4 Place value to 10 000+; all tables to 12×12; fractions & decimals
Y5 Place value to 1 000 000; multi-digit ×÷; fractions ↔ decimals ↔ %
Y6 Number to 10 000 000; long ×÷; fraction/decimal/% fluency; ratio/algebra intro
```

## Year 1

KS1 focus: confidence and mental fluency with whole numbers, counting, place value.

### Number and place value

- Count to and across 100, forwards/backwards from any number  
- Count, read, write numbers to 100 in numerals; count in 2s, 5s, 10s  
- Identify 1 more / 1 less  
- Represent numbers with objects / pictures / number line; language: equal to, more/less, most/least  
- Read and write numbers 1–20 in numerals and words  

**Depends on:** EYFS numbers to 10 + counting patterns.

### Addition and subtraction

- Read/write/interpret `+`, `−`, `=`  
- Represent and use **number bonds and related subtraction facts within 20**  
- Add/subtract one-digit and two-digit numbers to 20 (including 0)  
- Solve one-step +/− problems and missing-number problems  

**Non-statutory guidance:** memorise and reason with bonds to **10** and **20** in several forms (e.g. 9+7=16; 16−7=9).

**Depends on:** EYFS composition / bonds to 10.

### Multiplication and division (early)

- One-step ×÷ problems with objects, pictures, arrays (teacher support)  
- Doubling; simple fractions of quantities via sharing  

**Depends on:** counting in 2s/5s/10s; addition fluency.

### Fractions

- Half as 1 of 2 equal parts; quarter as 1 of 4 equal parts  

### Measurement / geometry (summary)

- Compare length, mass, capacity, time; coins; o’clock / half past  
- Name common 2-D / 3-D shapes; whole/half/quarter turns

## Year 2

By end of Year 2 (DfE): know bonds to **20**; precise place value; practise for fluency.

### Number and place value

- Count in 2s, 3s, 5s from 0; 10s from any number  
- Place value of each digit in a 2-digit number (tens, ones)  
- Compare/order to 100 using `< > =`  
- Read/write numbers to at least 100 in numerals and words  

**Depends on:** Y1 counting to 100 + bonds within 20.

### Addition and subtraction

- Bonds to 20 fluent; derive related facts to 100  
- Add/subtract: 2-digit+ones, 2-digit+tens, two 2-digit, three 1-digit  
- Commutative addition; inverse to check  

### Multiplication and division

- Recall 2, 5 and 10 tables; odd/even  
- Write ×÷ statements; commutative multiplication  

### Fractions

- 1/3, 1/4, 2/4, 3/4 of lengths/shapes/sets; equivalence 2/4 = 1/2  

### Measurement / geometry / statistics

- Standard units; money; time to nearest 5 minutes  
- Properties of 2-D/3-D shapes; position & direction  
- Pictograms, tally charts, block diagrams, simple tables

## Year 3

Lower KS2 focus: fluency with whole numbers and four operations; place value.

Highlights:

- Place value in 3-digit numbers; count in 4, 8, 50, 100  
- Column addition/subtraction up to 3 digits  
- 3, 4 and 8 tables (building on 2s via doubling)  
- Unit and non-unit fractions; count in tenths  
- Measure perimeter; tell time; angles as turns  

**Depends on:** Y2 place value + bonds + 2/5/10 tables.

---

## Year 4

DfE end-of-year expectation: **all multiplication tables to 12×12** memorised.

Highlights:

- Place value to 4-digit+; negative numbers (intro); rounding  
- Formal written +/−; ×÷ methods progressing  
- Factor pairs; multiply 2/3-digit by 1-digit  
- Fractions and **decimals** (tenths/hundredths)  
- Area; convert units; classify shapes; coordinates  

**Depends on:** Y3 tables 3/4/8 + column +/−.

| Skill ID | Skill | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `y4-tables-to-12` | All tables to 12×12 | Missing |

---

## Year 5

- Place value to 1 000 000; powers of 10  
- Multi-digit addition/subtraction; short/long multiplication; short division  
- Factors, multiples, primes, squares, cubes  
- Fractions: compare, +, −, ×; decimals to 3 places; **percentages**  
- Perimeter/area; volume; angles; reflection/translation  

**Depends on:** secure tables + fraction sense from Y3–4.

---

## Year 6

- Numbers to 10 000 000; formal long × and long ÷  
- Order of operations; mental calculation strategies  
- Fraction/decimal/percentage fluency; ratio and proportion  
- Simple algebra (one-step formulae / sequences)  
- Area of parallelograms/triangles; volume of cubes/cuboids  
- Circles (radius/diameter/circumference intro); pie charts; mean  

**Depends on:** Y5 multi-digit calculation + FDP equivalence.

---

## Suggested Primary Maths sequence (what to build next)

1. Reception: subitising + composition to 5  
2. Reception/Y1: deepen bonds to 10 (expand current activity)  
3. Y1: bonds within 20 + missing-number +/−  
4. Y1: counting / 1 more/less / read numbers to 20  
5. Y2: place value (tens & ones)  
6. Y2: 2, 5, 10 tables  
7. Y3–4: remaining tables → 12×12  
8. Fractions pathway in parallel once sharing/halves are secure  
9. Then decimals / percentages / multi-digit methods  

Do **not** jump to Year 4 tables or Year 5 percentages until the chain above is in place for that learner.
