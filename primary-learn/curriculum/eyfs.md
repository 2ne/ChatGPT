# Reception maths

Reception is the start of the path in [maths.md](./maths.md), not a separate curriculum.

The mathematics Early Learning Goals are covered by these categories:

| Early Learning Goal | Categories |
| --- | --- |
| Number — understanding to 10, composition, subitising to 5, bonds to 5, some bonds to 10, doubles | `cat-r-to-5`, `cat-r-to-10` |
| Numerical patterns — compare quantities to 10, odds and evens, equal sharing, count beyond 20 | `cat-r-to-10`, `cat-r-count-on` |

Compare quantities comes after counting to 10 and before composing numbers to 10. Shape, repeating patterns and measures are Development Matters, not ELG nodes. Add 2 or 3 extra Reception nodes for those later. Not now.

Node 1 is `eyfs-count-5`. Literacy and the other EYFS areas of learning are out of scope. This file is maths only. Each node still has Easy / Medium / Hard in the app.
