# EYFS / Reception pathway

Source: EYFS statutory framework (Early Learning Goals summarise expected development by the **end of Reception**).  
ELGs are assessment checkpoints, not a full day-by-day curriculum — but they tell us the **exit skills** before Year 1.

## Priority for Primary Path

Focus interactive practice on:

1. Mathematics (Number + Numerical patterns)  
2. Literacy (Word reading → then comprehension / early writing supports)  
3. Communication and language (vocabulary that unlocks everything else)

## Mathematics ELGs

### Number (must be secure before rushing past 10)

Children at expected level will:

- Have a **deep understanding of numbers to 10**, including the **composition** of each number  
- **Subitise** (recognise quantities without counting) up to 5  
- Automatically recall number bonds up to **5** (including subtraction facts) and **some bonds to 10**, including **double facts**

**Building-block order**

1. Subitise 1–5  
2. Count reliably within 10  
3. Composition of numbers to 5 (e.g. 5 is 2+3, 4+1, 5+0)  
4. Bonds to 5 + related subtraction  
5. Composition / bonds towards 10 + doubles  
6. Compare quantities within 10 (more / less / same)

### Numerical patterns

- Verbally count **beyond 20**, noticing the counting-system pattern  
- Compare quantities up to 10  
- Explore patterns within numbers to 10: odds/evens, doubles, equal distribution

**Why this matters:** Year 1 number bonds to 10 and counting to 100 sit on top of this. Weak composition to 10 → fragile KS1 maths.

## Literacy ELGs

### Word reading (phonics foundation)

- Say a sound for each letter of the alphabet and **at least 10 digraphs**  
- Read words consistent with phonic knowledge by **sound-blending**  
- Read aloud simple sentences/books matched to phonics, including some **common exception words**

### Comprehension

- Retell stories in own words; use new vocabulary  
- Anticipate key events where appropriate  
- Use vocabulary in discussion and role-play

### Writing

- Write recognisable letters (mostly correctly formed)  
- Spell by identifying sounds and writing letters for them  
- Write simple phrases/sentences others can read

**Building-block order**

1. Oral vocabulary & listening  
2. Letter–sound correspondence (GPCs)  
3. Blending for reading / segmenting for spelling  
4. Digraphs + common exception words  
5. Simple captions → sentences  
6. Early comprehension of what is read/heard

## Communication and language (supports every subject)

### Listening, attention and understanding

- Listen attentively; respond with comments/questions  
- Make comments about what they hear; ask questions  
- Hold conversation when engaged in back-and-forth exchanges

### Speaking

- Participate in small group / class / 1:1 discussion  
- Offer explanations for why things might happen  
- Express ideas/feelings using full sentences (with modelling/support)
