Primary Path maps `Reception` … `Year6` in app types. Reception content should reference EYFS / Early Learning Goals (ELGs), not KS1 programmes of study.

## Subjects by stage

### EYFS (Reception) — seven areas of learning

**Prime areas**

1. Communication and language  
2. Physical development  
3. Personal, social and emotional development  

**Specific areas**

4. Literacy  
5. Mathematics  
6. Understanding the world  
7. Expressive arts and design  

For this app, the interactive priority is **Literacy** and **Mathematics**, with Understanding the world feeding later science / history / geography.

## The building-block rule

At primary level, **later skills depend on earlier ones**. Example (maths):

```text
counting & subitising
    → numbers to 10 & composition
        → number bonds to 10
            → bonds to 20 + place value to 100
                → mental / written addition
                    → multiplication tables
                        → fractions / decimals / percentages
```

If a child skips bonds to 10, Year 2 facts to 20 and later column methods become fragile.

Same idea in English:

```text
listening & vocabulary
    → phonemes / graphemes
        → blending (reading) & segmenting (spelling)
            → fluency
                → comprehension & writing composition
```

## Recommended build order for Primary Path

1. **Maths number pathway** (EYFS → Y2 bonds / place value → tables → fractions)  
2. **English phonics + early reading / spelling**  
3. **Science** topic packs by year  
4. Broader foundation subjects as lighter knowledge quizzes  

Always prefer the **earliest missing prerequisite** over jumping to “impressive” later content.

## Year flexibility note (from DfE)

Schools must cover the programme of study by the **end of each key stage**, and may teach content earlier or later within a key stage. For Primary Path we still tag a **primary target year** so pathways stay clear for kids and parents.
