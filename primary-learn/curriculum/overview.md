# Curriculum overview (England) — maths

Primary Maths covers mathematics from Reception to Year 6. It does not teach other National Curriculum subjects.

## Stages and ages

| Stage | Year groups | Typical ages | Framework |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EYFS | **Reception** | ~4–5 | EYFS mathematics Early Learning Goals |
| Key Stage 1 | **Year 1**, **Year 2** | 5–7 | National Curriculum mathematics |
| Lower Key Stage 2 | **Year 3**, **Year 4** | 7–9 | National Curriculum mathematics |
| Upper Key Stage 2 | **Year 5**, **Year 6** | 9–11 | National Curriculum mathematics |

Reception content should reference EYFS mathematics Early Learning Goals, not KS1 programmes of study. See [eyfs.md](./eyfs.md) and [maths.md](./maths.md).

## The building-block rule

Later maths skills depend on earlier ones:

```text
counting & subitising
    → numbers to 10 & composition
        → number bonds to 10
            → bonds to 20 + place value to 100
                → mental / written addition
                    → multiplication tables
                        → fractions / decimals / percentages
```

If a child skips bonds to 10, Year 2 facts to 20 and later column methods become fragile.

## Build order

The learner path is the numbered nodes in `maths.md`: 45 categories, 124 quests, one after another. A learner starts at `eyfs-count-5` and cannot open a later quest until the one before it is mastered. Audit that list with `audit.md` before writing quests. Do not add other subjects.

## Year flexibility note (from DfE)

Schools must cover the mathematics programme of study by the **end of each key stage**, and may teach content earlier or later within a key stage. This app still teaches the statements in the order in `maths.md`, one category after another.
