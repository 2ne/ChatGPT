# English pathway (Reception → Year 6)

Source: [National curriculum English programmes of study](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-english-programmes-of-study) + EYFS literacy ELGs.

English is built on **spoken language**. Reading splits into **word reading** (phonics/decoding) and **comprehension**. Writing splits into **transcription** (spelling/handwriting) and **composition**.

## Master literacy pathway (building blocks)

```text
R  Listening, vocabulary, love of stories
R  Letter sounds → digraphs → blend to read / segment to spell
Y1 All 40+ phonemes; common exception words; simple sentences
Y2 Greater fluency; alternative GPCs; spelling patterns; grammar basics
Y3–4 Word reading mostly secure → comprehension depth; spelling appendix; paragraphs
Y5–6 Fluent reading across genres; advanced grammar/punctuation; extended writing
```

**Critical rule:** if decoding is weak, keep teaching systematic phonics urgently (DfE Year 1 guidance). Do not skip ahead to heavy comprehension-only tasks for those children.

## Year 1

### Word reading (phonics-first)

Pupils should be taught to:

- Apply phonic knowledge as the route to decode  
- Respond speedily to graphemes for **all 40+ phonemes** (including alternatives where taught)  
- Blend unfamiliar words with taught GPCs  
- Read common exception words  
- Read words with –s, –es, –ing, –ed, –er, –est endings  
- Read multi-syllable words with taught GPCs  
- Read contractions (I’m, I’ll…)  
- Read aloud books matched to phonic knowledge; reread for fluency  

**Depends on:** Reception letter sounds + blending.

### Reading comprehension

- Listen to / discuss poems, stories, non-fiction beyond independent level  
- Link to own experiences; retell familiar stories  
- Predict; infer from what is said/done; discuss title and events  
- Explain understanding of what is read to them  

### Spelling / transcription (summary)

- Spell words with taught 40+ phonemes; common exception words; days of the week  
- Alphabet names; –s/–es, un–, –ing/–ed/–er/–est where root stays the same  
- Handwriting: sit correctly; form lower-case, capitals, digits  

### Writing composition (summary)

- Say out loud what they will write; sequence sentences; re-read; discuss with others  
- Leave spaces; join words/clauses with `and`; begin capital letters, full stops, question marks, exclamation marks; capitalise `I` and names

## Year 2

- Continue phonics until decoding automatic; note alternative sounds  
- Read most words quickly/accurately without overt sounding when familiar  
- Comprehension: sequence, inference, prediction; discuss favourite words/phrases  
- Spelling: homophones, contractions, suffixes, common exception words (appendix 1)  
- Grammar: nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs; statements/questions/exclamations/commands; present/past; commas in lists; apostrophes  

**Depends on:** Y1 GPC fluency.

---

## Years 3–4

- Word reading: apply knowledge of root words, prefixes, suffixes  
- Comprehension: wide range of fiction/non-fiction/poetry; retrieve/record; infer; summarise; discuss language  
- Spelling: further prefixes/suffixes; homophones; possessive apostrophe; word list  
- Writing: plan, draft, evaluate; organise paragraphs around a theme; dialogue settings/characters  
- Grammar: wider conjunctions, adverbs, prepositions; present perfect; inverted commas; nouns/pronouns for cohesion  

**Depends on:** mostly fluent decoding from KS1.

---

## Years 5–6

- Word reading: morphology/etymology to support meaning  
- Comprehension: compare texts; evaluate author language; distinguish fact/opinion; recommend books  
- Spelling: end-of-primary word lists; silent letters; morphology  
- Writing: atmosphere, cohesion, précis, relative clauses, modal verbs, passive, colon/semi-colon/hyphen/dash/bullet points  
- Spoken language: debate, presentations, performances continue across the stage  

---

## Suggested Primary Path English sequence

1. Reception: single-letter sounds (interactive sound → letter)  
2. Reception: oral blending / segmenting games  
3. Reception/Y1: first digraphs + CVC reading  
4. Y1: full GPC set + exception words (small sets, mastery loops)  
5. Y1: caption/sentence reading with Check feedback  
6. Y2: alternative spellings + early grammar  
7. Then comprehension activities that still support weaker decoders with audio/read-aloud options  

Pick **one systematic phonics progression** (e.g. phases / school SSP) when we start building phonics activities, and stick to it in `skills` tags.
