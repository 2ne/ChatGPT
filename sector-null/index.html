HOW TO PLAY

Left thumb moves you and turns the gun that way. Right thumb fires — drag it to aim independently. Collect green emeralds for score before they fade. Use Recharge to swap your power cell.

Controller (Xbox / standard pad): left stick move, right stick aim and fire (Geometry Wars-style — push to shoot, release to stop), X or LB recharge, Menu pause. Menus use the D-pad or left stick, A to confirm, B to back. On Game Over, up/down spin the focused callsign reel, left/right move between letters (right past the last letter reaches Submit score), A confirms, B backs up.
